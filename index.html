Clipper Publisher

Clipper Publisher is a private tool that publishes short videos to the YouTube channels of its operator. The operator reviews and approves every video before it is scheduled. The tool then uploads the approved video to the operator's own channel at the scheduled time.

What it does with your Google account

  • Uploads videos (youtube.upload): uploads the operator's approved Shorts to the operator's own channel, scheduled with YouTube's publish time.
  • Reads recent uploads (youtube.readonly): lists the channel's latest uploads, only to check that a video was not already uploaded, so the same video is never posted twice.

The tool is not offered to the public. Only the operator authorises it, for channels the operator owns.
