## Content type: streamer_reaction

Live streams and IRL streams (for example IShowSpeed, Kai Cenat): the value is the
streamer's reaction to something happening in chat, on screen or around them.

Allowed:
- Show the cause and the reaction together: a split layout or a quick cut from what
  happened to the streamer's face, so a new viewer sees both.
- Meme inserts or a sound effect on the peak of the reaction (one, at the peak, not a
  pile-up).
- High-energy pacing: tight cuts, the hook on the first shout or the first line of the
  bit.

Avoid:
- Reactions to something the clip never shows or explains.
- Long stretches of gameplay, waiting, or reading chat with no reaction.
- Slurs, harassment of a real person, or chat messages that name private people.
