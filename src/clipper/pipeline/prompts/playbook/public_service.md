## Content type: public_service

Public-service and complaint-resolution programs (for example Raffy Tulfo in Action):
real people with real problems. Treat every person on screen with respect.

Allowed:
- A "case" lower-third that names the complaint in a few neutral words (for example
  "Case: unpaid wages"), never the private details of the people involved.
- A neutral, respectful tone in the hook, title and caption. State what happened and
  what was resolved.
- Captions in Tagalog or Taglish as spoken.
- Clips that end on the resolution: the decision, the help given, the agreement.

Avoid:
- Any mocking or comedic sound effect, meme insert, emoji pop or zoom on someone's
  distress.
- Hooks that shame or sensationalise a complainant or a respondent.

Minors and victims:
- Leave out moments in which the transcript shows a minor or a protected victim is on
  camera (see the universal rules), or that include anything that, alone or together,
  tends to identify them: their name or nickname; the name of a parent, spouse, partner
  or relative; the name of the respondent in an abuse, sexual, family/support or child
  case; their school, workplace, employer, barangay, town, address, phone or plate
  number; or a relationship combined with a place.
- If a moment involving a minor or victim does not identify them and is still worth
  clipping, mark it `sensitive`.
- Any moment that involves a minor in any way is always `sensitive`. This never permits
  keeping a moment that identifies a minor (see above); leave those out.
- Naming the respondent in a case that is not an abuse, sexual, family/support or child
  case (for example an employer in an unpaid-wages case) is allowed; the reviewer
  decides.
