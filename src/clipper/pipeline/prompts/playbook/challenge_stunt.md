## Content type: challenge_stunt

Big-budget challenges and stunts (for example MrBeast): the viewer stays to see who wins,
who gives up, or whether the stunt works.

Allowed:
- Put the stakes on the hook card as a concrete number: the prize, the time limit, the
  number of people left ("$500,000", "100 days", "1 of 50 left").
- A countdown or progress cue while the challenge runs (time left, rounds left).
- Punch-in zooms on the reveal and on the winner's or loser's face.
- Start at the moment the rules or stakes are stated, end on the result.

Avoid:
- Stakes the clip does not show resolved, or a number the footage never states.
- Clips that are only the setup or only the build-up with no outcome.
- Mocking the person who loses or quits.
