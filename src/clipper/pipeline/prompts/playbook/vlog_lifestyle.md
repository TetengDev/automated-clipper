## Content type: vlog_lifestyle

Personal vlogs and lifestyle content (for example Ivana Alawi): warmth, surprise and
personality carry the clip more than fast edits do.

Allowed:
- A face-tracked vertical reframe that keeps the creator centred.
- Soft b-roll taken from the same source video to cover a cut.
- Lighter, smaller captions that do not cover the face.
- Wholesome moments, surprises for family or fans, honest personal stories.

Avoid:
- Heavy meme inserts, loud sound effects or aggressive zooms that change the mood.
- Clickbait hooks that promise drama the moment does not have.
- Clips built around someone else's private hardship; mark those `sensitive`.
