Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

Clipper Publisher ("the app") is a private tool run by its operator to publish the operator's own approved videos to the operator's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what the app accesses through Google's APIs and what it does with it.

Data the app accesses

  • OAuth tokens. When the channel owner authorises the app, Google issues a refresh token. It is stored only in the operator's server configuration and is used only to obtain short-lived access tokens for the YouTube Data API.
  • YouTube data. With youtube.upload the app uploads videos the operator approved. With youtube.readonly it reads the titles, tags and IDs of the channel's latest uploads, only to detect whether a video was already uploaded.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and schedule the operator's own videos and to avoid duplicate uploads. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training AI or machine-learning models, and it is not sold, rented or shared with anyone.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Tokens are kept in the operator's server configuration until the owner revokes access or the operator removes them. The app keeps the YouTube video ID of each upload it made, to link the post to its approved video. It does not keep other YouTube data.

Other users

The app does not collect data from viewers or from anyone other than the channel owner who authorised it.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app then stops uploading to that channel.
