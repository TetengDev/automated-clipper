## Content type: comedy

Comedy vlogs and sketches (for example Cong TV): the clip lives or dies on timing.

Allowed:
- Sound effects placed on the comic beat (the pause, the punchline, the reaction), not
  sprinkled through the clip.
- Reaction zooms on the person who reacts to the joke.
- Emoji pops on the punchline, used sparingly.
- Start right before the setup line, end a beat after the laugh.

Avoid:
- Explaining the joke in the hook or title, or spoiling the punchline.
- Inside jokes that need earlier context to land.
- Jokes at the expense of a victim, a minor or a tragedy, even when the creator made
  them; leave those out or mark them `sensitive` with no effects.
